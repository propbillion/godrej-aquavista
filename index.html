50+ lifestyle amenities including an infinity pool and kids' pool, clubhouse, gym, pickleball, badminton and kabaddi courts, mini golf, pets park, yoga & meditation zone, senior citizen zone, games room, spa & sauna, mini theatre, library & business centre, indoor kids' play & crèche, BBQ area, cricket practice pitch, amphitheatre, sunset gallery and landscaped gardens.
